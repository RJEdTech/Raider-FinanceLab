Teaching Money in Excel

Money in Excel takes a student who has never opened a spreadsheet to building the sheets Personal Finance uses: a pay stub, a budget tracker, tax brackets, a credit card payoff and 40 years of compounding. Every build matches a FinanceLab tool, so students can check the sheet against the model.

What is on the page

  • Spreadsheet basics: 12 short lessons, each about 10 minutes. Lesson 10 here is money math (percent of, ROUND to the cent, growth); MathLab’s copy has a science lesson there instead.
  • Build it: 7 projects with up to three levels. Big picture walks through every step. Closer look writes one formula and fills it down with $ locks. Deep dive builds from a mostly blank sheet and charts it.
  • Every level: a gut guess first, then the build, Check my work against the model, a “watch it update” step, and Download as Excel file (a real .xlsx with the student’s formulas).

Time

Estimates for a first-time student working alone: a lesson, about 10 minutes; Big picture, 10 to 15 minutes; Closer look, 15 to 20; Deep dive, 25 to 35; opening and submitting the file the first time, 10 more. Students who finish early can redo a build with their own numbers in the downloaded file.

Canvas assignment text

Copy into a Canvas assignment with submission type File Upload (allowed type: xlsx).

Paycheck

Link: https://rjedtech.github.io/Raider-FinanceLab/excel.html#build-paycheck
Directions: Open the link and do Big picture, then Closer look. Guess first, build each line, press Check my work, then Download as Excel file. Open the file in Excel, change B1 to the salary from your Career Worksheet, and save it. Upload the .xlsx file here.
File they upload: paycheck.xlsx

Monthly budget

Link: https://rjedtech.github.io/Raider-FinanceLab/excel.html#build-budget
Directions: Do Big picture and Closer look. Then, in the downloaded file, replace column C with what you really spent last month (or your own estimate) and save it. Upload the .xlsx file and answer in a comment: which line was most over, in dollars and in percent?
File they upload: monthly-budget.xlsx

Tax brackets

Link: https://rjedtech.github.io/Raider-FinanceLab/excel.html#build-brackets
Directions: Do Big picture and Closer look. In the downloaded Closer look file, try three salaries (your career salary, $45,000 and $120,000) and write each one’s tax and average rate in a comment. Upload the .xlsx file.
File they upload: tax-brackets-closer.xlsx

40 years of investing

Link: https://rjedtech.github.io/Raider-FinanceLab/excel.html#build-investing
Directions: Do all three levels. Upload the Deep dive file. In a comment: at 10%, what share of the year-40 balance is money you put in yourself, and what does that tell you about starting early?
File they upload: forty-years-deep.xlsx

Credit card payoff

Link: https://rjedtech.github.io/Raider-FinanceLab/excel.html#build-creditcard
Directions: Do Big picture and Closer look. In the downloaded file, set the card to $1,000 at 20% with a $55 payment. Upload it and write the months and total interest in a comment.
File they upload: credit-card-payoff-closer.xlsx

Grade it fast

  1. Open the submitted .xlsx in Excel and press Ctrl+` (the key left of 1), or choose Formulas > Show Formulas. Every cell now shows its formula.
  2. Check three things: the key answer cells match the numbers in the build (they passed Check my work before download), the cells hold formulas rather than typed numbers, and the cells that must stay put use $ (Closer look and Deep dive).
  3. Press Ctrl+` again to go back. Changing an input (salary, rate, a spending line) and watching the totals update is a quick test that the formulas are live.

A 3-point rubric: 1 for correct results, 1 for formulas (not typed answers), 1 for the follow-up question in the comment (their own numbers, and what the result means).

Answer keys

Answer-key workbooks for each build are not posted here, so students cannot download them. Ask RJ Ed Tech for a copy.

Privacy

Everything runs in the student’s browser, with no logins. The only thing saved is which lessons are done, on that device. Nothing a student types is sent anywhere.
