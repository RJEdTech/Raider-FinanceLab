For teachers: how this works

Built for real decisions

Each tool follows the same four steps: the problem (a real case with real 2026 numbers), gut vs. math (students lock in a gut answer, work out one number themselves, then the math takes a turn and the tool shows the difference in dollars), your money runs on this (where this exact idea shows up in their life) and review (real-world and test-style questions with worked solutions).

Follow your course

Choose Personal Finance under Browse to see the tools in the order the RJ course covers them. Each unit page opens with a Why are we learning this? card you can project on the first day.

Teach, don’t preach

The tools show what the math says and leave the choice to the student. Nothing here is financial or tax advice, and every model says what it simplifies.

Three levels of detail

Big picture gives the main idea in everyday words. Closer look names the math and adds variables. Deep dive adds the formulas and harder questions. Any student can choose any level.

Put a tool in Canvas

Open any tool and use Embed this tool in Canvas at the bottom. Choose where it starts, copy the code and paste it into a Canvas page.

Privacy

Everything runs in the browser. There are no logins, and no student data leaves the device. An anonymous page counter (no cookies, no names) records which tools are opened and whether the visit came through a school device.
